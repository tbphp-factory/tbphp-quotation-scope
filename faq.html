Questions about comparing order quotations

Does a catalogue price settle the order scope?

No. The written quotation should identify the configuration, quantity and commercial conditions for the actual request. A catalogue can help identify a product family without answering all order-specific questions.

Is an empty cost field the same as an included item?

No. It may mean that the item was not considered. Keep the field marked as unknown and ask for clarification rather than entering zero.

Can two different quantities be compared directly?

They can be recorded side by side, but the quantity difference must remain visible. Ask for a consistent basis if the comparison is intended to support the same purchasing decision.

What if artwork changes after the quotation?

Identify the new revision and ask whether the price, scope or timing changes. Do not assume a previous quotation applies to files that the supplier has not reviewed.

Does this worksheet establish compliance?

No. It organizes commercial information. Product documentation, destination requirements and suitability need separate model-specific review by the responsible parties.
