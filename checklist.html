Quotation scope comparison checklist

Use this worksheet before comparing totals. Leave unknown items open until the supplier responds.

Request identity

Record the request revision, product reference, quantity, destination and whether the request is for samples, a first order or replenishment. Check that both quotations answer the same version.

Included and excluded work

Create separate entries for device configuration, cartridge assortment, packaging, artwork work and delivery arrangements. For each entry, record included, excluded or awaiting clarification. Do not assign a zero price to an unanswered field.

Evidence and timing

Keep the quotation reference, date, stated validity, accepted file revision and lead-time conditions together. Record the source of each clarification and the person who must review it.

Review handoff

Write a short explanation of the remaining differences. Identify the next question, its owner and the decision that cannot yet be made. Preserve superseded quotations as history while clearly marking the current version.
